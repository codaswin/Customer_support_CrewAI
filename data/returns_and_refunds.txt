BrewHaven Coffee - Returns and Refunds Policy

You can return unopened bags of coffee within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Opened bags can be returned within 14 days if you are not satisfied with the taste; we refund 50% of the price.

Brewing equipment (grinders, kettles, espresso machines) can be returned within 30 days if it is unused and in its original box. Customers pay return shipping unless the item arrived damaged.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days after we receive the return. To start a return, email returns@brewhaven.example with your order number.
